How to play

  • Base camp: look around with ← → (or drag), walk with ↑ ↓, and pack your two items with Space (or tap them).
  • The jungle: run with the arrow keys or WASD, sprint with Shift, and use your weapon with Space. Strike a zombie when it flashes a red ! for a perfect counter. Find the hidden airstrip.
  • The cockpit: race your rival to start your plane and take off before the zombies reach the airstrip (90 seconds).
  • Work the real controls: tap switches, drag the throttle knob in, hold the key, pull back on the yoke. On a computer you can also use the keys on the white tags.
  • Follow the checklist card in order. Out of order makes the engine sputter and costs 3 seconds.
  • Prime 2 or 3 times. Too much floods the engine and you have to wait.
  • Keep an eye on the fuel selector on the floor…
  • Stuck? After a few seconds the control you need starts to glow.
  • The flight: bank with ← →, set throttle with ↑ ↓, and follow the gold arrow. Stay out of the storm and away from birds. First through the finish gate wins.
