# Story script (approved 2026-11-03; character names are fictional)

Spoken lines are recorded in assets/voice/. Female characters use the female cloned voice, male characters the male cloned voice, both on Eleven v4. Players never see the real people's names.
The opening narration voice is still to decide (suggested: whichever character the player picked, spoken in first person after they choose; before that, shown as text only).

## 1. Opening (text on screen, over the jungle at dusk)

Kanoa Island. Four hundred miles from anywhere.

A fever carried by mosquitoes is spreading across the Pacific. No medicine stops it.

But Kanoa's jungle grows plants found nowhere else on Earth. So a small camp of botanists and jungle explorers came here with one job: find the cure before the fever finds everyone else.

For three years, they searched. They were close.

Then the mosquitoes on Kanoa changed.

The fever reached the camp. The ones it takes don't stay down. They get back up.

The supply boat isn't coming. The radio is dead.

But somewhere past the ridge, hidden under the canopy, there's an old dirt airstrip. And on it, a few small planes that still might fly.

Only one way off this island. Wheels up, before they find you.

## 2. Menu and characters

Menu: 1 player (practice) or 2 players (invite code; the other player joins on their own phone or PC). Each player then picks their own character:

- Female botanist: **Dr. Ivy Thorne** (white lab coat)
- Female jungle explorer: **Wren "Riptide" Calloway** (olive vest, rust bandana, rope and machete)
- Male botanist: **Dr. Elias Fernsby** (silver hair, green cardigan, bow tie, carries a fern)
- Male jungle explorer: **Kip "Compass" Monroe** (safari gear, carries a frog)

## 3. Choose your path

### Dr. Ivy Thorne, The Botanist
Dr. Ivy Thorne came to Kanoa to study the moonflower orchid, a plant that only blooms in the dark. Her last test showed something nobody expected: its nectar slows the fever. She has the only sample in her lab coat pocket. She also has a pilot's license she earned to reach research sites no road goes to. Now she has to use it.

Spoken line (female voice): "I have the only cure on this island in my pocket. I'm not leaving it here."

### Wren "Riptide" Calloway, The Jungle Explorer
Wren earned the name Riptide the day she swam a flooded river to pull a guide out of it. She has led expeditions through eleven jungles and never lost a guest. She guided the cure team to every sample they ever found. She knows every trail on Kanoa, she can read a river like a map, and she flies bush planes into strips most pilots won't touch. Tonight her whole team is gone, and she's not about to be next.

Spoken line (female voice): "I know these trails better than anyone. Let's go. Now!"

### Kip "Compass" Monroe, The Jungle Explorer
They call him Compass because he has never once known which way north is. Kip has been lost in fourteen jungles and found his way out of every single one, usually with a new friend and a better story. He came to Kanoa to guide the scientists to a rare tree frog whose skin might hold part of the cure. He got the frog. He also got chased by zombies. He still has the frog.

Spoken line (male voice): "Good news, I found the frog. Bad news, everyone else found us."

### Dr. Elias Fernsby, The Botanist
Dr. Elias Fernsby talks to his ferns, and he swears they talk back. He came to Kanoa to grow the cure: a jungle fern whose sap might teach the body to fight the fever. His greenhouse is the last building with its lights still on. When the first zombie walked out of the trees, his ferns were the first to notice. He grabbed his favorite one and ran.

Spoken line (male voice): "Okay, little guy. We're getting on that plane."

## 4. Base camp: pack your bag

Everyone starts in the same base camp at dusk (a first-person view of a photoreal camp: lab tent, greenhouse, campfire, supply crates, river dock). Walk around and find your two items, then pack them in your flight bag:

- Dr. Ivy Thorne: the moonflower sample and her aviation headset
- Wren "Riptide" Calloway: the trail map and her machete, Mildred
- Dr. Elias Fernsby: his favorite fern and his seed journal
- Kip "Compass" Monroe: the frog (in its jar) and his camera

Other people's things are in camp too; you can only pack your own. Your rival packs in the same camp, so it doubles as the waiting room before the run. Your heart pounds while you search.

After packing, run for the airstrip through the jungle. Both paths end at the airstrip: the cockpit race begins.

## 5. Effort lines during the race (same voice as the chosen character)

Engine catches: "Come on... come on, come on!"
Mistake: "No, no, no! Not now!"
Zombies at the glass: "Whoa!"
Airborne: "We're up... we made it."
